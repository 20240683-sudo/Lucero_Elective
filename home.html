MANOLO FORTICH

Manolo Fortich is a first-class municipality in Bukidnon, Northern Mindanao. Known as the gateway to the province, it sits about 40 minutes from Cagayan de Oro City. The town is famous for its cool climate, scenic landscapes, and rich agricultural land.

Manolo Fortich is home to Del Monte Philippines, one of the country’s biggest pineapple plantations. It also offers top tourist attractions like Dahilayan Adventure Park, Dahilayan Forest Park, and Mt. Kitanglad Range Natural Park, perfect for adventure and nature lovers.

With its growing economy, active community, and strong educational institutions like Northern Bukidnon State College, Manolo Fortich continues to develop while keeping its peaceful and welcoming atmosphere.
